  25. VI. Calculation of Tax Amount (based on the Tax Amount Table for 2026)
  26. The amount of income tax and special income tax for reconst ruction withheld from salary, bonuses, etc. can be determined by using the Tax Amount Table.
  27. If the payer of salary, etc. calculates payments of salary, etc. by computer or other office machinery, the amount of income tax and special income tax for reconstruction withheld from the salary , etc. to which
  28. column 甲(kou) applies in the monthly table may be determined using the method prescribed by the Minister of Finance (public notice by the Ministry of Finance).
  29. 1 How to Use Column 甲(kou) of the Monthly Table (if an "Application for Exemption for Dependents of Employment Income Earner" is submitted)
  30. (Example)
  31. (1) Payment amount of salary, etc. (monthly amount) 420,000 yen (2) Social insurance premiums deducted from salary, etc. 63,087 yen (3) Number of dependents 2 persons
  32. (Spouse covered by a withholding exemption and one additional relative qualified for withholding deduction)
  33. [Tax calculation] If spouses fall under persons with disabilities, one person is added.
  34. [page 29]
  35. (1) Social insurance premiums, etc., are deducted from the salary: 356,913 yen (420,000 yen – 63,087 yen)
  36. (2) In the column headed "amount of salary, etc. after deduction of social insurance premiums, etc. , for the month"（その月の社会保険料控除後の給与等の金額）in the monthly table, find the row for "356,000 or
  37. more and less than 359,000," which includes 356,913 yen, then read across to column 甲(kou) headed "2 dependents." You will then find the withholding amount of 7,020 yen in the box where the row and
  38. column intersect. This is the amount of income tax and special income tax for reconstruction withheld from salary, etc.
  39. [Entries in the ledger of withholding tax] Monthly payments of salaries, etc., the amounts of social insurance premiums and withheld taxes are recorded
  40. in the "Ledger of Withholding Tax for Employment Income"（給与所得に対する源泉徴収簿）and used for the year-end adjustment.
  41. 2 How to Use Column 乙(otsu) in the Monthly Table (if an "Application for Exemption for Dependents of Employment Income Earner" is not submitted)
  42. (Example)
  43. (1) Payment amount of salary, etc. (monthly amount) 80,750 yen (2) Social insurance premiums deducted from salary, etc. none [Tax calculation]
  44. (1) As there are no social insurance premiums to be deducted from the salary , etc. , the amount paid of 80,750 yen is the amount of salary, etc. after deduction of social insurance premiums.
  45. (2) In the column headed "amount of salary, etc. after deduction of social insurance premiums, etc. , for the month," find the row for "less than 105,000 yen," which includes 80,750 yen. This will be the "amount
  46. equivalent to 3 .063% of the salary , etc. after deduction of social insurance premiums" in column 乙 (otsu). Therefore, 2,473 yen (80,750 yen  3.063%, any fraction of less than one yen is omitted ) is the
