  13. VII. Year-end Adjustment The y ear-end adjustment is th e process whereby the payer of salary, etc. settles the discrepancy for each
  14. person between the total amount of income tax and special income tax for reconstruction withheld from each payment of salary, etc. made during the year and the tax amount to be paid for total salary, etc. paid during the
  15. year at the time of the last payment of salary, etc. in the year. For most people receiving a salary , etc. , therefore, the year-end adjustment plays an important role as a substitute for filing a final return.
  16. 1 Timing of the Year-end Adjustment The year-end adjustment is, in principle, made at the time of the last payment of salary, etc. in a year. In the
  17. case of any person who has ceased working during the year due to death or who has become a non-resident due to a transfer to an overseas branch, the year-end adjustment is performed at the time of the person's death
  18. or transfer to an overseas branch.
  19. 2 Persons Not Covered by the Year-end Adjustment Salaries, etc. paid to the following persons are not subject to the year-end adjustment.
  20. (1) Persons having neither domicile in Japan nor residence for one year or more in Japan (non-residents)
  21. (2) Persons who do not submit an "Application for (Change in) Exemption for Dependents of Employment Income Earner" (to which column 乙(otsu) or 丙(hei) of the tax amount table applies)
  22. (3) Persons receiving payments of salary, etc. during the year in the amount of 20,000,000 yen or more (4) Persons for whom collection has been suspended or who have received a tax refund relating to
  23. withholding income tax and special income tax for reconstruction for their salaries, etc. during the year under the provisions of the Act on Exemption, Reduction or Postponement of Collection of Taxes for
  24. Disaster Victims (5) Persons who have left their jobs during the course of the year (excluding those who have ceased working due to death, etc.)
  25. 3 Salary Covered by the Year-end Adjustment The amount of salary, etc. subject to the year-end adjustment is the amount of salary, etc. which should be
  26. paid when the date of payment arrives between January 1 and December 31 of the relevant year. Therefore, the amount of salary, etc. which should be paid during the year shall be included in the yearly salary , etc. and
  27. subject to the year-end adjustment, even if unpaid.
  28. 4 Procedures for the Year-end Adjustment For practical information on how to perform the year-end adjustment, see "Year-end Adjustment Guide", in Japanese only posted on the website of the NTA （https://www.nta.go.jp）.
  29. VIII. Payment of Withheld Income Tax and Special Income Tax for Reconstruction Withheld income tax and special income tax for reconstruction is to be paid by the 10 th day of the month
  30. following the month in which the salary, etc. was paid (in cases where exceptional permission is received, by July 10 and January 20 in the following year) by sending a "data on the statement of income tax collected" via
  31. e-Tax (cashless payment), or paid at a local financial institution or over the counter at the competent tax office, together with submission of a "Statement of income tax collected (tax payment slip)".

  33. Even if no tax is to be paid, the statement of income tax collected (payment slip) should be submitted to the competent tax office; or should be submitted over the counter in the competent tax office. (In this case, the
  34. data on s statement of income tax collected" can also be sent via e-Tax.)
  35. For detailed information, please see the NTA website,"Procedures for Payment of Withholding Income Tax" [https://www.nta.go.jp/users/gensen/nencho/index/gensen_nouzei/cashless.htm].
