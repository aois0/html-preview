  17. individual number or corporate number of the payer and the recipient with respect to each recipient of the remuneration/fees, contract money, etc. and submit it , together with a totalized table , to the district

  25. corporate number of the payer and the recipient (only for an individual or a corporation having an individual

  30. 5 Points to be Noted When Preparing Withholding Record, etc.
  31. (1) A payer of salary, etc. or retirement allowance, etc. is required to state the individual number or corporate
  32. number of the beneficiary, etc. in the copy of a withholding record submitted to the district director of the competent tax office, but shall not state the individual number or corporate number of the beneficiary, etc.
  33. in the copy of a withholding record issued to the beneficiary, etc.
  34. (2) The copy of a report of payment issued to a recipient cannot be issued stating the individual numbers of the payer and the recipient in it.
  35. (3) When submitting a withholding record or report of payment to the district director of the competent tax office, if the number of records or the number of reports that should have been submitted in the second
  36. preceding year is 30 or more, they must be submitted via e-Tax, using cloud service, etc., or using optical disks, etc.
  37. (Note) For information return to be submitted before December 31, 2026, those with 100 or more pages that should have been submitted two years prior must be submitted via e-Tax, etc.
  38. (4) If a payer of salary, etc., retirement allowance, etc. or remuneration/fees and other payments (1) does not submit a withholding record or a report of payment to the district director of the competent tax office
  39. before the due date or submits with false statements or records, or (2) does not issue a withholding record to the beneficiary before the due date or issues with false statements (provid es false records using
  40. electromagnetic means), the payer shall be punished by imprisonment with required labor for not longer than one year or a fine of not more than 500,000 yen.
  41. III. Relief for Disaster Victims (1) If the property of a person who receives salary, etc., remuneration/fees, public pension, etc., suffers great
  42. damage caused by an earthquake, storm, flood, lightning, or fire, he/she may be granted a postpone of collection or refund of withholding tax under the Act on Exemption, Reduction or Postponement of
  43. Collection of Taxes for Disaster Victims (hereinafter referred to as the "Disaster Exemption Act").
